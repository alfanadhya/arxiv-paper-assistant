4Simulations on recorded scores

No new runs; re-scored from the saved results.

  • Sending up to 10 papers while scores stay ≥ 0.97: would recover almost nothing (0 in the title run, 1 in the question run), at about twice the context.
  • N+2 with Qdrant’s top 2 in the +2 slots: predicted +1 (PPO) and no losses, which the later runs confirmed. In practice it fired often but changed no outcome.

5Overall picture

  • Retrieval is strong when the query keeps the paper’s own words: full titles, abstract-like questions, or a correct filter.
  • The main remaining weakness is the reranker on crowded topics. For famous papers it scores the original and its follow-ups alike, so a top-5 cut loses the original. A title-match signal is the fix suggested by the title= route’s 22 / 22.
  • The model sometimes shortens a title into a generic query, and once (DPO) chose a look-alike paper from the right candidates. The rewritten query instruction reduced the shortening but did not stop it.
  • The input checks are reliable.
